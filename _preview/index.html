FAQs

Good questions, quick answers

Still unsure? Our team is a phone call away.

How do bulk discounts work?

Discounts apply automatically per item: 15% off from 5 items, 20% from 10, 25% from 20, 30% from 50 and 35% from 100 items.

What printing method do you use?

We use DTF (Direct to Film) printing with commercial-grade inks. It produces vibrant, detailed prints that stay flexible and durable on a wide range of fabrics.

What artwork files can I upload?

We accept EPS, SVG, AI, PDF, PNG and JPG files up to 10MB. Vector files give the sharpest result. Returning customers can use artwork already on file.

Can you design a logo for me?

Yes. Choose the new logo design option when ordering or mention it in your enquiry and our team will work with you on a design.

Where can I print my logo?

Popular locations include left chest, right chest, centre front, centre back, lower back and both sleeves. Each location is priced on the product page.

Do you deliver outside Melbourne?

Yes. We are based in Melbourne's South East and dispatch orders to customers right across Australia.
